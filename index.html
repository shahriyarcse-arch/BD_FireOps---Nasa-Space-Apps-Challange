What is this?

One clean fire record for the Bangladesh hills.

Two NASA satellites counted fires here: MODIS (2002–2011, sees big fires) and VIIRS (2012–2021, sees small fires too). If we join them directly, fires look like they almost tripled overnight — but only the camera changed. BD-FireOps fixes that jump, so 20 years can be compared fairly.

Please read · Honest limits

What this is NOT

  • Not a live fire alarm. History and research only.
  • Only for these 3 hill districts — not the whole world.
  • No dot can mean clouds hid the fire. Missing dot is not proof of no fire.
  • Does not tell why a fire started.
  • Night rows included: day-only check moves results ~10% — baseline keeps both, honestly reported in the repo docs.

For the judges · How we did it

Five steps, nothing hidden

  1. Got real NASA data — daily fire dots for our box, 2002–2021, from the FIRMS archive.
  2. Kept the right dots — only afternoon (Aqua) MODIS, only inside the 3 hill-district borders, low-confidence rows dropped.
  3. Counted by month — empty months inside coverage stay 0; months with no coverage stay blank.
  4. Fitted one line — learned on 2012–2018 (both cameras flying), then checked on 2019–2021, which the model never saw.
  5. Showed the proof — wrong join vs fixed line above, test numbers in the proof cards, and every source open in the repo.
