Region-Specific Scope

BD-FireOps is fitted specifically to the Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT), Bangladesh. It is an empirical prototype, not a universal global formula. Transferring these coefficients to Boreal or Amazonian biomes requires local recalibration. It estimates a harmonized detection count only — it is not a fire-cause classifier.

Detection Realities

NASA FIRMS reports positive thermal detections. Non-detection does not prove absence of fire; heavy monsoon cloud cover (June–October), dense smoke, or orbit overpass gaps can obscure active ground fires.

Operational Transition

Suomi-NPP VIIRS delivery officially terminates on November 1, 2026. Future operational models must transition to NOAA-20 (VJ114) and NOAA-21 (VJ214) sensor streams.
