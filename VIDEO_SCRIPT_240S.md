## 🗣️ Natural Speaking & Pronunciation Guide

1. **Speak like a teammate, not a commercial:** Use a warm, confident, friendly tone — like you're showing a cool engineering project to an interested professor.
2. **Numbers made easy:**
   - Say *"twenty twenty-six"* (not two thousand twenty-six).
   - Say *"fifty-five point six seven"* (not fifty-five point sixty-seven).
   - Say *"ninety-five percent"* (crisp and clear).
   - Say *"NOAA-twenty and NOAA-twenty-one"* (NOAA is pronounced like *"Noah"*).
   - Say *"FIRMS"* like the word *"firms"*.
   - Say *"VIIRS"* like *"veers"*.
   - Say *"MODIS"* like *"MO-dis"*.
   - Say *"Jhum"* like *"Jhoom"*.
3. **Pacing:** If you pause for half a second when transitioning between sections, it makes the video feel polished and easy to follow. Don't rush!
